Privacy Policy

2048 Panda Group

Effective Date: January 1, 2025  |  Last Updated: February 21, 2025

Welcome to 2048 Panda Group ("the Site," "we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, and what rights you have over your data. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect two categories of information: information you provide directly, and information collected automatically when you use the Site.

a) Information You Provide Directly

2048 Panda Group does not require registration or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information through the game itself.

b) Automatically Collected Information

When you visit the Site, certain information is collected automatically by web servers and third-party services. This may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system and device type
  • Pages you visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring URLs (the page you came from before visiting us)
  • Date and time of your visit
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level only)

This data is collected primarily through server logs and Google Analytics and is used in aggregate form to understand how visitors interact with the Site.

2. Local Storage (Browser Data)

To save your best game score between sessions, 2048 Panda Group stores a small piece of data in your browser's localStorage. This data:

  • Is stored only on your own device
  • Is never transmitted to our servers
  • Does not contain any personally identifiable information
  • Can be deleted at any time by clearing your browser's site data

The key stored is 2048-xiongmaotang-best-score and its value is a plain number (your high score).

3. Cookies

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a website. We use two categories of cookies:

a) Essential Cookies

These are strictly necessary for basic website operation (e.g., security tokens, load balancing). They do not track you across other websites and cannot be disabled without breaking essential Site functionality.

b) Third-Party Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)

To help cover hosting and development costs, we display ads on the main game page through Google AdSense. Google AdSense uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Show ads that are relevant to your interests based on your browsing history
  • Cap ad frequency and measure ad performance
  • Prevent fraudulent ad clicks

Google may use the DoubleClick cookie and the NID cookie (and others) to personalize ads. These cookies are set by Google's ad-serving infrastructure and are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can review or opt out of interest-based advertising at any time through Google Ads Settings or via the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.

Ads are only displayed on the main game page (index.html). The About page, Privacy Policy page, and 404 page contain no ad code whatsoever.

4. Google Analytics

The Site may use Google Analytics to collect anonymized usage statistics. Google Analytics uses cookies to track sessions, pages viewed, and user behavior (in aggregate). We use this data solely to understand traffic patterns and improve the game experience.

Google Analytics data is anonymized — IP addresses are truncated before storage. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to deliver the Site. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Fonts – Loads our web fonts (Fredoka One, Poppins) from Google's servers. Google may log your IP. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • jsDelivr CDN (Font Awesome) – Loads icon fonts. See jsDelivr Privacy Policy.
  • Tailwind CSS CDN – Loads our CSS framework. Requests go to Tailwind's CDN servers.
  • Google AdSense – Serves advertisements on the game page. See above and Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third parties beyond those listed above.

6. Children's Privacy

2048 Panda Group is designed to be a fun, family-friendly game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction under COPPA, GDPR-K, or equivalent laws). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

Parents should be aware that Google AdSense may serve personalized ads to users of all ages unless Google's child-directed treatment settings are enabled. You can configure ad personalization settings through Google Account settings.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access – Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification – Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure – Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to Restrict Processing – Ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Right to Object – Object to data processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to Data Portability – Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • CCPA Rights (California residents) – Know, delete, and opt out of sale of personal information

Since we collect minimal personal data, most of these rights can be exercised by clearing your browser's local storage. For rights related to third-party services (Google Analytics, AdSense), please contact those providers directly.

8. Data Retention

We do not maintain a database of individual user records. Server log files that may contain IP addresses are automatically purged after 30 days. Your browser's local storage data (best score) remains until you clear your browser data — it is never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we handle, including using HTTPS encryption for all connections. However, no internet transmission or storage system is 100% secure. We encourage you to use up-to-date browsers and security software when navigating any website.

10. Links to Other Websites

The Site may contain links to external websites (e.g., Google's privacy policy pages). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites and encourage you to review their individual privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in law, technology, or our practices. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Site after any change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us. We aim to respond to all inquiries within 5 business days.

You can reach us through the contact information listed on the About page.
