2048 Panda Group

About the Game

2048 Panda Group is a free, browser-based merge puzzle game inspired by the original 2048 created by Gabriele Cirulli in 2014. Instead of plain numbered tiles, every tile in this version displays a real photo of a Panda Group fan community member — making each merge feel like leveling up a character.

The game is played on a 4×4 grid. You slide tiles using arrow keys or touch swipes. When two tiles of the same value collide, they merge into one tile with double the value. The goal is to reach the legendary 2048 — Grand Master tile, unlocking the final and most powerful member of the Panda Group.

Every session is different. Your best score is automatically saved to your browser so you can track your personal records over time. No sign-up, no account, no downloads — just open the page and play.

  • 11 unique Panda Group member tiles (values 2 through 2048)
  • Undo up to 10 moves to recover from mistakes
  • Local best-score persistence via browser storage
  • Responsive design — works on desktop, tablet, and mobile
  • Keyboard (arrow keys) and touch (swipe) controls
  • Zero ads on non-game pages — clean and distraction-free

About the Creator

This game was built by a casual developer and Panda Group enthusiast who wanted to blend the addicting strategy of 2048 with the fun, familiar faces of the Panda Group community. The project started as a weekend experiment and grew into a polished, fully playable game over several iterations.

The creator has a background in front-end web development and takes special interest in small browser games, interactive media, and creative fan projects. The member photos used in the tiles are sourced with respect for the original content creators and their communities.

If you enjoy the game, the best way to support the project is simply to share it with friends. No donations are requested — just spread the word and enjoy the game!

Contact: For questions, feedback, bug reports, or takedown requests, please reach out via the contact form or email. All reports are reviewed within 5 business days.

How It's Built

2048 Panda Group is built with a deliberately lightweight stack — no heavy frameworks, just clean HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. This keeps the game fast, accessible, and easy to maintain.

HTML5 Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+) Tailwind CSS (CDN) Google Fonts Font Awesome 6 LocalStorage API

The game logic is implemented in a single game-v2.js class-based module. The rendering uses the DOM directly without a virtual DOM layer, keeping frame performance smooth. Touch support is handled natively with the Touch Events API.

All member images are stored locally alongside the HTML, so the game loads instantly without depending on any external image CDN. The entire project is deployable as a static site to any hosting provider.

Our Mission

We believe that simple, fun games should be freely accessible to everyone. 2048 Panda Group will always be free to play. There are no in-app purchases, no pay-to-win mechanics, no pop-ups, and no tracking beyond what is described in our Privacy Policy.

We are committed to keeping the game update-friendly, adding new features and optimizations based on player feedback. Community input genuinely shapes future versions of the game.
