Complete Guide: How to Play 2048 Panda Group

2048 Panda Group is a number-merging puzzle game inspired by the classic 2048. The twist? Every tile is a real photo of a Panda Group member. Your goal is to keep merging until you unlock the legendary Grand Master (2048) tile. Every merge scores points, and the best scores are saved to your browser.

Step-by-Step Rules

# Action What Happens Tip
1 Start the game Two random tiles (value 2 or 4) appear on the 4×4 board Your first move sets the direction — think before swiping!
2 Swipe or press arrow keys All tiles slide in that direction as far as possible Use ↑ ↓ ← → on desktop; swipe on mobile
3 Two equal tiles collide They merge into one tile with double the value — and you earn those points! Only one merge per tile per move
4 New tile spawns A new tile (2 or 4) appears in a random empty cell after every move Keep the board from filling up by merging frequently
5 Hit Undo Reverts to your previous board state (up to 10 steps) Use undo sparingly — save it for mistakes
6 Reach 2048 You unlock the Grand Master tile and win! The game continues so you can go even higher
7 Board fills up If no moves remain, the game ends Always try to keep at least one corner reserved for big tiles

Pro Strategy Tips

🏔️ Corner Strategy
Keep your highest tile in one corner. Build around it in a snake pattern.
🚫 Avoid One Direction
Try not to swipe in all four directions randomly — pick 2-3 preferred directions.
🔗 Chain Merges
Build chains so one swipe causes multiple merges — this maximizes your score.
